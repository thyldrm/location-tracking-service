Worker: what one ping can do

Idempotent by construction

A redelivered ping fails the watermark test, and even if it passed, INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING and DELETE ... RETURNING decide what is written.

Failure outside the picture

A transient error (database or network down) aborts the batch: it is redelivered after back-off and lag grows. Other errors retry 3 times, then go to the dead-letter topic.

Known blind spot

Boundary jitter is not filtered: a user flapping at an edge creates many entries.
